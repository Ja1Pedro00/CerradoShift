# CerradoShift 🌱🛰️

**Smart Agriculture Powered by NASA Data**

CerradoShift is an open-source agricultural decision-support platform designed to help farmers, researchers, and agricultural professionals make data-driven decisions about crop rotation, soil conditions, and climate risks.

Focused on Brazil's Cerrado biome, the platform combines NASA Earth observation data, environmental datasets, geospatial analysis, and scientific models to evaluate agricultural conditions and support more sustainable farming practices.

## Key Features

- **NASA Data Integration:** Historical climate information, precipitation, temperature, and satellite observations.
- **Soil Diagnostics:** Soil texture, pH, organic matter, and water availability assessments using available geospatial sources or user-provided measurements.
- **Crop Rotation Analysis:** Scientific models supporting the evaluation of crop rotation strategies.
- **Climate Risk Assessment:** Analysis of drought conditions, water deficits, and environmental risks.
- **Vegetation Monitoring:** Satellite-based NDVI and EVI analysis.
- **Interactive Dashboard:** Agricultural indicators, geographic data, and visual reports.
- **Regional Data Integration:** Brazilian agricultural datasets, SoilGrids, and MapBiomas integrations.

## Technology Stack

**Frontend:** React, JavaScript, Vite

**Backend:** Python, FastAPI

**Data Sources:** NASA POWER, NASA HLS, ISRIC SoilGrids, MapBiomas, Open-Meteo, and other geospatial services.

**Scientific Computing:** Python-based environmental analysis, agricultural calculations, and crop rotation models.

## Project Goal

To make satellite-derived environmental information and scientific agricultural models more accessible, helping users compare farming strategies, identify potential risks, and promote sustainable land management.

**Developed for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 — Field Shift: Adapting Farms with NASA Data.**

*Note: Some external data integrations require configuration or authentication. CerradoShift provides preliminary decision support and does not replace laboratory soil testing or professional agronomic recommendations.*
